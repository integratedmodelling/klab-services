## Construction and proof

MonotoneRefinementSupport registers completed named expression profiles at the end of the actual
SemanticsBuilder and at the existing-class typed declaration return. Profiles contain detached
normalized unchanged structure and canonical attribute identifiers. Both directions are considered
when a new profile arrives. Equal attribute counts and a bijective authoritative subtype proof
prevent dropped occurrences, sibling replacement and accidental shared substitutions. Repeated
cached declarations cause no pair-proof work. Staging records profiles but defers DL-dependent
proof until the complete candidate is available.

The resulting SubClassOf(new,original) has an annotation property
`urn:klab:construction:attribute-refinement`, with derived provenance and the captured local knowledge
generation. It is stored only in k.derived. No equivalent-class axiom or authored observational
containment permission is added. The exported OWL direction is available to ordinary native-bearer
proof; model validation and dependency eligibility remain separate gates.

Provider checks happen outside the OWL monitor. Publication checks the profile epoch and semantic
generation again, then imports/flushes the private closure and checks consistency plus endpoint
satisfiability. Insertion, import, first flush and final validation share rollback protection.
A failed batch retracts its owned axioms and invalidates query caches. Helper and OWL lifecycle
operations use OWL-before-helper nested locks, matching synchronized OWL reset. Retraction
advances its proof epoch and clears service caches/revision even if native removal or flush fails.
An uncertified native closure retires that semantic actor: declaration, subsumption, matching and
native reasoner queries fail closed until a fresh actor is accepted. This includes a warmed positive
answer followed by authority denial and an injected retraction-flush failure.

## Cache and lifecycle boundary

Derived closure changes clear semantic query, concept/observable projection, hierarchy-distance,
subsumption and syntactic-match caches without advancing the accepted worldview revision. Existing
runtime semantic commitments therefore do not invalidate themselves merely by constructing a
specialization. Source updates discard profiles and owned links; authority changes retract owned
links, retain profiles for re-proof, advance the existing authority knowledge revision and clear
queries. The next declaration or explicit refresh performs re-proof; arbitrary old-object queries
can conservatively remain unresolved until then. Actor publication retains the existing accepted
SemanticEnvironment serialization; this helper is not a new cross-service synchronization layer.

Derived export is a snapshot of that semantic environment. Its provenance is not authorization to
reuse authority-dependent proofs against another provider state. No long-lived classification or
observation membership policy is inferred from these axioms.
