## What primary sources establish — and do not establish

Paper DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0281348` states the showcased outputs use default
50 m resolution and **mean climate values for May 2018**. Publisher supplement
S3A specifies `rg`, `rg2`, `rgc_max` in **MJ/m²**, with `fr` in **[0,1]**; S3B gives
25 for each of ten classes. These are stronger constraints than guessed labels.
They do not establish whether the recovered radiation raster represents a daily
mean over May or a monthly-integrated amount.

Conflicting evidence must remain visible:

- Archived PaL data model declares input `MJ`, not the dependency's `MJ/m²`.
- Recovered WCS range metadata says `W.m-2.Sr-1`; TIFF units are absent.
- PuertoClima.R explicitly converts its own arrays to `MJ/m2/d`; that does not
  authorize dividing this different PaL payload by 1000.
- The supplement pairs **FestucaRubra (Agrostis curtisii)** in S3B and other tables.
  The recovered legacy ontology's explicit GBIF key resolves to **Festuca rubra**.
  These are not an automatically interchangeable scientific-name mapping.
- Supplement parentheticals also narrow Calluna to Calluna vulgaris and
  Helictotrichon to Helictotrichon cantabricum; the recovered ontology uses genera.

Do not add an unapproved PAR spectral conversion factor or silently reinterpret
the 25 threshold. Ask whether the named PAR threshold describes total incident
radiation at maximum photosynthetic capacity or actual spectrally restricted PAR,
and confirm that both sides of the comparison have the same temporal support.

The stored `fr` result must retain the published dimensionless [0,1] fraction.
Do not multiply it by 100 merely because the legacy observable says percentage.
Review any modern proportion/percentage mapping with explicit stored-value,
transport-unit and display conventions before implementation.

The article's data-availability statement describes its PaL dataset as AGPL v3
and generated by the authors. Confirm that the current production coverage is
that dataset/version and covered by that statement before redistributing bytes.
No payload has been published by this work.

## Proposed biological compatibility definitions (English, not executable k.WV)

The legacy evidence is `integratedmodelling/im@b15c8fe7c9c2aaedc1ea3c83e67f7e18716f91a3`
(2026-09-22), **not an attested 2023 execution worldview**. The current source
comparison is `imod@1328000fff63a1951465b1f7b130c0af15264c95`. Candidate names below
are provisional review labels. None is a declared class or approved equivalence.

| Legacy reference | Legacy kind/meaning | Candidate direction and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `earth:SolarRadiation` | Energy; total solar radiation | Reuse existing name if quantity dimensions and temporal support are admitted. An unchanged name alone does not validate MJ/m² versus MJ or radiance. |
| `im:Maximum` | Child of DataReduction, applies to Quantity; may trigger transformations | `data:Maximum` is a review candidate because both declarations concern numeric reduction/aggregation. Confirm the fixed threshold is not accidentally turned into a runtime maximum over some spatial/time window. No automatic prefix rename. |
| `im:Relevant` | Deniable attribute, with legacy comments noting subjective relevance | Provisional project attribute **SelectedForPaLClassification**: identifies the selection encoded by the archived vegetation legend, without asserting dominance, cover fraction or a generic ecological ranking. Producer must confirm this reading. |
| `landcover:Pastureland` | Land-cover identity, with CORINE 231 and other scheme metadata | Provisional **PaLPastureContext**, limited to this study's managed-pasture context. Review its bearer/kind in the compound Plant observable. Do not equate every shrub/fern category with CORINE 231 or create a global crosswalk. |
| `biology:Plant` | Agent specializing legacy Individual through Plantae identity | Provisional **PlantIndividual** scoped to living individual plants, possibly specializing current `life:Individual` after taxonomy review. A classified raster cell is not thereby one organism, and classification must not create presence observations. |
| `ecology:Vegetation` | Identity specializing LifeForm | Provisional **VegetationLifeForm** preserves the identity reading. It is not automatically a Subject, Community, biomass stock or a collective Agent. Any changed bearer for the limiting factor must be explicitly reviewed. |
| `biology:Growth` | Biological process affecting individual biomass during Life | Provisional **PlantBiomassGrowth**: biological growth process, not generic physical expansion or observed positive growth. `fr` limits potential growth; it does not execute the growth process. Do not import the entire legacy Life-event chain merely to make a name resolve. If necessary constraints cannot be retained in this bounded scope, stop for re-scoping. |
| `earth.incubation:PhotosyntheticallyActiveRadiation` | Energy derived from SolarRadiation causing legacy Photosynthesis within Location | Review reuse of the current `biology:Photosynthesis` process and a project threshold observable **RadiationAtMaximumPhotosyntheticCapacity**. This is not approval to equate all solar radiation with PAR or to assume a wavelength-band conversion. |
| `ecology.incubation:Overstory` | Attribute under VegetationStratum | Provisional **PaLOverstoryStratum** applied to the classification of the upper vegetation layer; not a taxon, height measurement, canopy fraction or proof of woody cover. Keep main/overstory classifications distinct even where their resulting identities are equal. |

A review must return explicit accepted definitions and unresolved alternatives,
not just approval of these labels. Avoid wholesale `im`→`imod` substitutions.
If the biological bearer/constraint migration is larger than this subset, the
stop rule applies; no broad ontology project is implicitly authorized.

**Recommended review policy:** treat the original vegetation legend as an
operational classification with ten distinct entries; maintain separately
reviewed taxonomic links. This avoids pretending an ambiguous legend label is an
authoritative species observation. It is a proposed semantic adaptation, not
permission to replace the archived taxonomic models without review.

Required equality invariant: nine common main/overstory codes remain exact-equal
only for their matching entry; the other 81 of 90 possible valid code pairs are
different. Subsumption/common ancestry must not replace `==`. In particular,
Festuca species and Gramineae/Poaceae family remain different legend entries even
though taxonomically related. No missing code is silently made an “other” class.

## Input binding and reference plan — proposed, not executed

1. Retain original archive/file hashes and alternate production-endpoint receipt.
   Obtain the producer's quantity/time/version statement before any conversion.
2. Choose a target grid explicitly. The actual native degree-based main-vegetation
   grid is a useful proposed adapted-pilot fixture; it is NOT automatically the
   paper's model-selected “50 m” grid. Paper-exact comparison requires the original
   target transform/CRS or independent exported reference.
3. Require categorical-safe handling. Inspected geospatial source defaults
   CATEGORIZATION to nearest-neighbor and numeric MEASURE to bicubic, with WCS
   override support. Verify the actual loaded binary and observable classification;
   float32 storage alone does not establish the correct interpolation. Bicubic
   continuous output can overshoot; do not clamp without approval.
4. Do not hide missingness. Native-point input QA in the current small crop finds
   324 main-cell centers, 288 with all three inputs present and **36 with an input
   missing**. All 288 jointly valid pairs have equal codes. Thus this crop alone
   does **not** test the attenuation branch. This is input availability QA, not
   Java execution or approved resampling.
5. Select an additional bounded mixed-class patch before computation. Full-array
   same-index QA finds 23054 differing vegetation pairs; three candidate points
   were independently checked against containing overstory cells. First candidate:
   longitude -3.6670247765205177, latitude 43.48527765926827, main code 21, overstory
   7. No radiation/reference result has yet been fetched at that candidate point.
   Whole-array counts are not a proof of global mask/grid equivalence.
6. After D1–D4, prepare expected fields from the approved input transformation and
   the independent Puerto equations, using existing scientific libraries rather
   than porting Puerto. Verify all 90 legend equality cases plus zero, threshold,
   saturation, missing/unmapped input and taxonomically-related-unequal cases.
   Agree on missingness behavior rather than choosing null==null or zero by default.
7. Pre-register value tolerances after dtype/unit/interpolation contracts are fixed.
   Review proposal: rg2 absolute bound max(4 float32 ULPs, 1e-6 in the approved
   energy/area unit); fr absolute 1e-6. Class IDs, masks, bounds, transform and units
   must meet exact declared contracts separately. No tolerance may conceal wrong
   classification, time support or grid alignment. Existing pure-expression
   oracle tolerance 1e-12 remains unchanged for its separate scalar checks.
8. Proposed later execution cap: two small contexts, no more than three requested
   outputs each, one bounded attempt with owned cleanup, no automatic write replay.
   Record exact Java/worldview/component hashes, source model/result association,
   actual inputs, units/time/masks and independent value comparison. No installs,
   shared changes or resource writes without their explicit approvals.

## Stop rule

Stop before live execution if the named radiation product's units/time cannot be
authoritatively established, code 7's interpretation remains unresolved for the
claimed semantic scope, or category/bearer migration expands into broad ontology
work. Also stop for an unimplemented Java/component contract, unsupported import
or missing permissions; do not fix those covertly. Re-scope explicitly rather than
manufacturing a scientific pass. A legend-only arithmetic demonstration must be
labelled as such, not promoted to the authentic semantic PaL pilot or P4.
